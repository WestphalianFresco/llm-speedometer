CLUTCH
Hold the clutch to shift.

Shifting

  1. Click the clutch pedal. It stays down — or hold Space to work it like a real one.
  2. Drag the lever up its slot, across the middle, and down into the gear you want. It will not cut the corner.
  3. Click the pedal again. The gear takes as the pedal comes up.

Try to shift with the clutch up and it grinds, as it should.
